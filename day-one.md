# Day One at ShopTruck

Welcome to the team. Read this before your first task.

## What we do

We sell truck parts and car parts through our own website and on eMAG. Our customers range from fleet operators and independent mechanics to everyday car owners and dealers. What sets us apart is the breadth of our catalog and the depth of our expertise.

## How things work

- **Website** — custom-built with Next.js.
- **ERP** — our own custom system handles inventory, orders, and operations.
- **Payments** — processed through EUPlatesc.
- **Invoicing** — handled by SmartBill.
- **eMAG** — our second sales channel alongside the website.

## Team structure

We operate with dedicated roles: warehouse, customer support, and development. You will work alongside people who own their area — ask them how things flow before assuming.

## Rules that matter

1. **Never ship without testing.** No exceptions.
2. **Ask for permission before modifying anything.** If you are about to change data, configuration, code in production, or anything shared — get approval first.

## Your first week

Success in week one means one thing: drive as many users as possible to the new platform. Understand the product catalog, learn the tools, and focus your energy on growth.
